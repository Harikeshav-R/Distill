Architecture

Dual-stream intelligence

Two models run in parallel in your browser. No data ever leaves your device.

01
Importance scoring
A Llama-3.2 transformer runs a single forward pass, assigning every token an importance probability from 0 to 1.
02
Entity anchoring
A parallel BERT model identifies critical entities (names, dates, numbers, technical terms) and forces 100% retention.
03
Strategic pruning
Tokens below your threshold are removed. Union-of-masks fusion ensures no hallucinations. Under 200ms total overhead.
